03 / The realization

You were doing more than the résumé said.

Meaning comes from actual experience, not a flattering guess.

One defensible interpretation
Structured technical investigation with a clear handoff

A small résumé bullet can conceal the work of finding evidence, narrowing a cause, and knowing where your responsibility ends.

Supporting statement — illustrative
“The execution plan showed a full table scan. I sent the query, slow logs and my notes to the database team.”
What this doesn't claim

No claim that the candidate implemented the database fix, selected a production change, or measured the final performance result.

Why it matters

The candidate can describe investigation and escalation more precisely in their own words.
